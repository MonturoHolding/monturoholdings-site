About Monturo Holding LLC

A family enterprise built on structure, intention, and multi-generational stewardship.

Our Story

Monturo Holding LLC was founded in January 2026 by Arturo D. Rodriguez and Monica W. Rodriguez as the governing backbone of the Rodriguez family enterprise. What began as a decision to operate with more structure — separating personal assets from business activity, formalizing governance, and creating a framework built to last — became the foundation of everything we do.

Rather than reacting to opportunities one at a time, we chose to build the infrastructure first: entities, documentation, governance. The holding company exists not for complexity's sake, but to simplify decision-making, protect what matters, and make it easier for current and future family members to understand how everything fits together.

Mission

To steward family capital — financial, relational, and intellectual — through structures and systems that are intentional, transparent, and built to last across generations.

Our Structure

The Rodriguez family enterprise operates through a layered entity structure designed to separate risk, clarify ownership, and protect long-term assets.

Rodriguez Family Revocable Living Trust

The governing trust established January 2026. Holds real estate and serves as the ultimate ownership layer of the enterprise. Co-trustees: Arturo D. Rodriguez & Monica W. Rodriguez.

Monturo Holding LLC

The parent holding company. Governs entity relationships, allocates capital, and maintains the family enterprise framework. 100% owned by the Rodriguez Family Revocable Living Trust.

Monturo Notary & Signing Services LLC

Subsidiary operating company providing mobile notary, remote online notarization (RON), and loan signing services across St. Clair County and Metro East Illinois. 100% owned by Monturo Holding LLC.
