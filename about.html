About Monturo Holding LLC

Monturo Holding LLC was created to give the Rodriguez family enterprise a durable, organized, and legally distinct backbone. Instead of reacting to opportunities one at a time, we choose to operate from a written framework: entities first, documentation first, governance first.

The holding company is not about complexity for its own sake. It exists to simplify decision-making, protect what matters, and make it easier for current and future family members to understand how everything fits together.

Mission

To steward family capital—financial, relational, and intellectual—through structures and systems that are intentional, transparent, and built to last across generations.

What we value

  • Clarity: Simple, documented structures over unwritten assumptions.
  • Separation: Clear lines between personal, holding, and operating activity.
  • Resilience: Planning for downside first, upside second.
  • Education: Making the system teachable to the next generation.
  • Privacy: Thoughtful exposure and information control by design.
