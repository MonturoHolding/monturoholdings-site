Portfolio & structure

Monturo Holding LLC operates as the central holding entity for current and future projects, companies, and assets. Some entities are active, others are planned, and all are evaluated within a consistent governance and risk framework.

Current focus areas

Operating businesses

Family-owned and closely held businesses operating under separate LLCs, each with its own bank accounts, records, and risk boundaries.

Real estate & future assets

A growing focus on long-term, income-producing assets, acquired and held through appropriately structured entities.

Intangible & digital assets

Domains, digital infrastructure, and systems that support the enterprise, documented and managed as real components of the portfolio.

Entity structure (high-level)

At a high level, Monturo Holding LLC sits above individual operating LLCs, with each entity maintaining its own records, agreements, and bank relationships. The holding company focuses on alignment, oversight, and capital allocation, not day-to-day operations.

Detailed organizational charts and legal documents are maintained privately and are not published online.
