Portfolio & Structure

A disciplined, layered enterprise — entities and operating businesses built to compound over time.

Enterprise Structure

The enterprise is organized through a layered entity structure designed to separate risk, protect assets, and create a clear governance framework for current and future generations.

Governing Layer

Family Trust

The governing trust serves as the ultimate ownership layer of the enterprise, holding real estate and providing continuity across generations. Established under Illinois law with spendthrift, incentive, and family governance provisions.

▼
Holding Layer

Monturo Holding LLC

The parent holding company and family enterprise hub. Governs subsidiaries, allocates capital, and maintains the governance framework. 100% owned by the Family Trust. Registered in Illinois.

Real Estate

Real estate assets are held within the family trust structure, providing asset protection and seamless estate planning continuity. Future acquisitions will be evaluated for optimal entity placement prior to closing.

Acquisition Framework

Future asset acquisitions are evaluated through a disciplined framework emphasizing long-term value, risk separation, and multi-generational relevance.

Risk separation

Each asset or venture is structured in its own entity to contain downside and protect the broader enterprise.

Tax efficiency

All acquisitions are evaluated for tax-aware ownership structure before closing.

Multi-generational lens

We ask: does this asset make sense in 10 years? 30 years? Can it be passed on clearly?
