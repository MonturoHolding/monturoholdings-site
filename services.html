Core functions & services

Monturo Holdings LLC exists to centralize governance, oversight, and long-term planning for the Rodriguez family enterprise. While each operating company has its own mission and risk profile, the holding company provides a stable, consistent framework.

Holding company functions

Entity design & oversight

Defining how new LLCs, partnerships, and joint ventures are formed, documented, and integrated into the existing structure.

Policy & governance

Maintaining written policies for decision rights, approvals, distributions, risk thresholds, and communication across entities.

Capital allocation

Evaluating opportunities through a consistent lens, balancing growth potential with downside protection and liquidity needs.

Recordkeeping & compliance

Coordinating documentation, minutes, resolutions, and key records to keep the enterprise audit-ready and orderly.

Who we work with

Monturo Holdings primarily serves internal stakeholders: family members, closely held entities, and trusted advisors. Select external relationships may exist where interests are aligned and governance expectations are clear.
