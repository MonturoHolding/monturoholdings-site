Monturo Holding LLC

Holding & Governance

Monturo Holding LLC is not a client-facing business. Its mandate is internal: govern the family enterprise, allocate capital, and maintain the systems that allow operating subsidiaries to run cleanly.

Entity Structuring

Formation, governance, and ongoing management of LLCs, trusts, and operating ventures within the family enterprise framework.

Asset Oversight

Long-term acquisition, management, and review of real estate, businesses, and financial assets with a multi-generational ownership lens.

Governance Systems

Written policies, documented decision rules, and communication structures designed to be teachable, repeatable, and audit-ready.

Subsidiary Management

Oversight and support of operating subsidiaries including compliance tracking, banking coordination, and annual reporting.

Notary & Signing Services

Professional notary and document signing services across St. Clair County and Metro East Illinois. Both traditional and remote online notarization (RON) available.

Mobile Notary

We come to you — home, office, hospital, or anywhere you need a notary in St. Clair County and surrounding Metro East Illinois communities.

Remote Online Notarization (RON)

General Notarizations
